Good questions

Frequently asked questions

Is the quote really the final price?

Yes. Your written quote lists the exact cost, materials and timeline, and the final invoice matches it. If anything unexpected comes up, it’s documented and approved by you before we proceed.

How long will my project take?

You’ll get specific start and finish dates with your quote, not a range. Most interior projects take 2–5 days; exteriors depend on size and prep. The same crew works your project every day until it’s done.

Do I need to move my furniture or cover anything?

No. Our crew moves belongings to the centre of the room and wraps them. Floors are covered, edges taped, hardware removed and reinstalled, and the work area cleaned daily.

What paint do you use?

Premium paint systems from Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, Dulux and Cloverdale Paint, matched to your surfaces and conditions.

How do I know the finish will meet the standard?

Every project ends with a final walkthrough, and the job isn’t finished until you’ve signed off. Every project is also backed by a 3-year written workmanship warranty.

What areas do you serve?

We’re based in Coquitlam and serve homeowners and businesses across the Tri-Cities (Coquitlam, Port Coquitlam and Port Moody) and the surrounding Lower Mainland.
